name: Technical Explanation
description: Explain how a mechanism works and why it exists
appliesTo: concept, architecture, implementation, overview
---

Explain the mechanism, not the slogan. Say what problem this section solves, what happens internally, and which terms a developer must get right. Use a short mental model. Skip phrases like "in today's world", "revolutionizing", and "leverage". If research does not establish a claim, say it is unverified.
