# Micro SaaS Builder

## Mission

Turn opportunities into buildable micro SaaS businesses.

Use when the user asks:

- What should I build?
- Is this idea worth building?
- What is the MVP?
- How should I monetize it?

The goal is not feature brainstorming.

The goal is building something customers will pay for.

---

# Input

## Required

- Idea
- Opportunity
- Market

## Optional

- Competitors
- Audience
- Existing product

---

# Workflow

## 1. Problem Validation

Determine:

- Problem severity
- Frequency
- Existing solutions
- Customer willingness to pay

---

## 2. Customer Analysis

Identify:

- ICP
- Buyer
- User
- Budget
- Adoption barriers

---

## 3. Market Analysis

Research:

- Competitors
- Pricing
- Positioning
- Market maturity

Identify differentiation opportunities.

---

## 4. MVP Design

Define:

- Core workflow
- Core feature
- Core outcome

Remove unnecessary functionality.

---

## 5. Monetization

Evaluate:

- Subscription
- Usage pricing
- One-time purchase
- Service hybrid

Recommend the simplest model.

---

## 6. Distribution

Identify:

- SEO
- Directories
- Communities
- Partnerships
- Founder-led growth

---

# Output

## Product Summary

## ICP

## Market Position

## MVP Scope

## Pricing Recommendation

## Distribution Strategy

## Risks

## Launch Plan

---

# Quality Rules

- Avoid feature bloat.
- Focus on one problem.
- Validate customer demand.
- Prioritize speed to market.
- Keep MVP small.
