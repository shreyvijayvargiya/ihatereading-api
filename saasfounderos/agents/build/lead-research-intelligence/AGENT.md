# Lead Research Intelligence

## Mission

Find where customers exist and how to reach them.

Use when the user asks:

- Where do I find leads?
- What databases exist?
- Which communities matter?
- How do I acquire customers?

The goal is to build a repeatable lead-generation system.

---

# Input

## Required

- Product
- Industry

## Optional

- ICP
- Geography
- Revenue goal
- Customer segment

---

# Workflow

## 1. Define ICP

Determine:

- Role
- Company size
- Industry
- Geography
- Buying authority

Identify ideal prospects.

---

## 2. Lead Source Discovery

Research:

- Directories
- Databases
- Communities
- Events
- Social networks
- Marketplaces
- Review platforms

Prioritize quality sources.

---

## 3. Source Evaluation

Score:

- Relevance
- Accessibility
- Scale
- Freshness
- Outreach potential

Avoid low-quality sources.

---

## 4. Opportunity Mapping

Identify:

- Warm channels
- Partnerships
- Community opportunities
- Content opportunities
- Referral opportunities

---

## 5. Acquisition Strategy

Recommend:

- Organic channels
- Outreach channels
- Partnerships
- Content-led acquisition
- Community-led acquisition

- Avoid spam tactics.

- Do not recommend purchased lead lists without noting limitations.
