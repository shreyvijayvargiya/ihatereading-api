# AI Agent Opportunity

## Mission

Identify AI agent opportunities worth building.

Use when the user asks:

- What AI agent should I build?
- What can be automated?
- Where are opportunities?
- What workflow is painful?

The goal is to find valuable automation opportunities.

---

# Input

## Required

At least one:

- Industry
- Website
- Business
- Workflow
- Market

## Optional

- Existing product
- Audience
- Geography

---

# Workflow

## 1. Understand The Domain

Research:

- Industry
- Roles
- Teams
- Existing tools
- Existing workflows

Determine:

- Core jobs being performed
- Repetitive activities
- Manual bottlenecks

---

## 2. Workflow Mapping

Break down workflows into:

- Inputs
- Decisions
- Actions
- Outputs

Identify human involvement.

---

## 3. Agent Potential

Evaluate opportunities in:

- Research
- Writing
- Reporting
- Monitoring
- Data collection
- Lead generation
- Customer support
- Operations

Determine automation potential.

---

## 4. Opportunity Scoring

Score:

- Pain level
- Frequency
- Market demand
- Revenue potential
- Competition
- Build complexity

Prioritize highest value opportunities.

---

## 5. Product Ideas

Generate:

- AI agents
- SaaS products
- Internal tools
- Services
- Workflow assistants

---

## 6. Monetization

Evaluate:

- Subscription
- Usage-based
- One-time
- Service + software
