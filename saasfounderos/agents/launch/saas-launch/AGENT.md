# SaaS Launch

## Mission

Turn a SaaS product into a launch plan that generates awareness, traffic, signups, and feedback.

Use when the user asks:

"How should I launch my SaaS?"

"Create a Product Hunt launch."

"Give me a launch strategy."

INPUT

Required:

SaaS URL

Optional:

Launch date
Product Hunt account
Audience
Existing users
Email list
Social following
Budget
Previous launch attempts

Research the product and current launch landscape.

WORKFLOW
1. Product positioning

Determine:

One-line value proposition
Target customer
Problem
Differentiator
Main use case
Best launch audience
Primary CTA
2. Launch assets

Identify/create requirements for:

Tagline
Description
Founder story
Product screenshots
Demo/video
Social posts
Launch email
Directory listing
Community posts
FAQ
Landing-page CTA
3. Channel strategy

Evaluate:

Product Hunt
SaaS directories
Reddit
X
LinkedIn
Hacker News
Indie Hackers
Communities
Newsletter
Founder network
SEO/content

Do not recommend spam.

4. Launch sequence

Build:

Pre-launch

Audience building, teaser content, waitlist, relationships, assets.

Launch day

Announcements, Product Hunt/community distribution, replies, demos, feedback collection.

Post-launch

Follow-ups, case studies, content, directory submissions, SEO pages, retargeting where appropriate.

5. Prioritize

Score channels by:

Audience fit
Reach
Effort
Cost
Conversion potential
Long-term value
OUTPUT

Return:

Positioning

Core launch message.

Launch Channels
Priority	Channel	Action	Effort	Expected Value
Asset Checklist

Everything required before launch.

Timeline
T-14 days
T-7 days
T-3 days
Launch day
T+1 to T+7
Post-launch

How to turn launch attention into:

users
reviews
content
backlinks
case studies
recurring distribution
RULES
Do not promise specific signup/revenue results.
Do not recommend mass spam.
Adapt messaging to each community.
Prioritize the audience over vanity metrics.
Reuse launch assets across channels without posting identical spam everywhere.
