# Market Intelligence

## Mission

Turn a market, industry, category, or product space into actionable intelligence.

Use when the user asks:

* Is this market worth entering?
* Who are the competitors?
* What opportunities exist?
* What are customers looking for?
* How crowded is this space?
* What trends are emerging?
* What should I build?

The goal is not to gather information.

The goal is to help the user understand where opportunities and risks exist.

---

# Input

## Required

At least one:

* Website URL
* Product URL
* Company
* Competitor
* Industry
* Category
* Market
* Topic

## Optional

* Target audience
* Geography
* Customer segment
* Existing competitors
* Existing product
* Business goal

Ask only for information that cannot reasonably be researched.

---

# Workflow

## 1. Understand The Market

Determine:

* Market category
* Industry
* Primary customer
* Buying process
* Major use cases
* Common problems
* Existing solutions

Define the market clearly before researching competitors.

---

## 2. Competitor Landscape

Identify:

### Direct Competitors

Products solving the same problem.

### Indirect Competitors

Alternative approaches and substitute solutions.

### Emerging Competitors

New products, startups, and fast-growing entrants.

For each competitor capture:

* Product
* Positioning
* Pricing model
* ICP
* Distribution strategy

Do not assume the largest company is the most important competitor.

---

## 3. Customer Demand

Research how customers discuss the problem.

Use available sources such as:

* Search results
* Reddit
* Communities
* Reviews
* Forums
* Social platforms
* Industry discussions

Identify:

* Pain points
* Desired outcomes
* Buying triggers
* Frustrations
* Objections
* Switching reasons

Separate evidence from assumptions.

---

## 4. Market Structure

Determine:

### Market Maturity

* Emerging
* Growing
* Mature
* Saturated

### Customer Segments

Identify important audiences.

Examples:

* Startups
* Agencies
* Enterprises
* Developers
* Creators
* Ecommerce
* SMBs

### Positioning Patterns

Map common positioning used across competitors.

Examples:

* Cheapest
* Fastest
* Enterprise
* AI-first
* Easiest
* Premium
* Specialized

---

## 5. Trend Analysis

Identify:

* Emerging trends
* New technologies
* Market shifts
* Customer behavior changes
* Regulatory changes where relevant
* Distribution shifts
* AI-related opportunities

Distinguish between:

### Current Trends

Actively visible today.

### Emerging Signals

Potential future opportunities.

Avoid trend speculation without evidence.

---

## 6. Opportunity Discovery

Identify opportunities in:

### Product

Missing products, features, workflows, or customer segments.

### Content

Topics competitors fail to cover.

### Distribution

Channels competitors underutilize.

### Pricing

Packaging and pricing opportunities.

### AI

Automation, AI agents, workflows, and AI-first experiences.

### GEO / AI Search

Visibility opportunities in AI answer engines.

---

## 7. Risk Analysis

Identify:

* Strong incumbents
* Regulatory risk
* Platform dependency
* Distribution challenges
* High acquisition costs
* Customer adoption barriers
* Market saturation

Do not exaggerate risks.

Explain likelihood and impact.

---

## 8. Strategic Positioning

Determine potential positions such as:

* Budget
* Premium
* Enterprise
* AI-first
* Vertical-specific
* Audience-specific
* Workflow-specific

Recommend positions supported by evidence.

Do not invent differentiation.

# Quality Rules

* Do not fabricate market size.
* Do not fabricate customer opinions.
* Do not fabricate competitor pricing.
* Distinguish facts from assumptions.
* Prefer evidence over speculation.
* Do not recommend opportunities that lack product-market relevance.
* Focus on actionable insights rather than information overload.
* When evidence is limited, clearly state uncertainty.

---

# Core Principle

The objective is not to describe the market.

The objective is to help the user answer:

> "Where is the opportunity, what is the risk, and what should I do next?"
