# Competitor Intelligence

## Mission

Turn competitors into actionable business intelligence.

Use when the user asks:

- Who are my competitors?
- What are they doing better?
- How are they positioned?
- What are their weaknesses?
- Where can I compete?
- How should I differentiate?

The goal is not to collect competitor information.

The goal is to identify opportunities and threats.

---

# Input

## Required

- Website URL
- Competitor URL or name

## Optional

- Industry
- Market
- Geography
- Customer segment

---

# Workflow

## 1. Understand The User Product

Research:

- Product
- ICP
- Features
- Pricing
- Positioning
- Distribution

Determine:

- Why customers choose this product
- Who the ideal customer is
- Current market position

---

## 2. Research Competitors

Capture:

- Product
- Features
- Pricing
- Messaging
- Audience
- Business model
- Distribution channels

Identify:

- Direct competitors
- Indirect competitors
- Emerging competitors

---

## 3. Customer Sentiment

Research:

- Reviews
- Reddit
- Communities
- Forums
- Social discussions

Find:

- Complaints
- Praise
- Switching triggers
- Missing features
- Buying motivations

Separate facts from assumptions.

---

## 4. Positioning Analysis

Determine how competitors position themselves:

- Cheapest
- Fastest
- AI-first
- Enterprise
- Developer-focused
- Agency-focused
- Premium
- Specialized

Identify positioning gaps.

---

## 5. Competitive Advantage Analysis

Compare:

- Features
- Pricing
- Distribution
- Customer experience
- SEO
- GEO/AI visibility

Identify:

- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Defensible advantages
- Vulnerabilities

---

## 6. Strategic Opportunities

Discover:

- Product opportunities
- Marketing opportunities
- Distribution opportunities
- Pricing opportunities
- AI opportunities

Prioritize based on impact.

- Do not invent reviews.
- Do not invent pricing.
- Do not invent customer sentiment.
- Clearly separate evidence from assumptions.
- Focus on actionable intelligence.
