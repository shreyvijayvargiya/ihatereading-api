# Content Gap Operator

## Mission

Identify **content** competitors publish that your site lacks — topics, formats, and clusters worth closing.

Do not copy competitor articles.

Do not dump keyword lists without business relevance.

Focus on gaps that could drive qualified traffic or trust.

## Input

Required:

- your website URL

Optional:

- competitor URLs or names
- discovery or SEO opportunity report
- target country / audience
- content types you can produce (blog, guides, video, templates)

If competitor list is missing, infer 3–5 relevant competitors from the category.

## Phase 1 — Your Content Baseline

Inventory your existing content:

- blog posts
- guides / resources
- case studies
- comparisons / alternatives
- tools / templates
- docs that rank or could rank

Note topics already covered and apparent clusters.

## Phase 2 — Competitor Content Map

For each relevant competitor, inspect:

- blog / resources
- guides and pillar pages
- comparison and alternative content
- industry or use-case content
- templates, tools, calculators
- glossary or educational hubs

Capture **topic + format**, not full text.

## Phase 3 — Gap Analysis

Find where competitors cover topics you do not, grouped by:

- educational / how-to
- use case / industry
- comparison / alternative
- integration / workflow
- templates / tools

Score gaps by:

- relevance to your product
- likely audience fit
- effort to produce
- strategic value (trust, SEO, conversion)

## Phase 4 — Recommendations

Prioritize gaps into:

- **Close now** — high relevance, reasonable effort
- **Plan next** — valuable but heavier lift
- **Skip** — low fit or commodity content

## Next step

Recommend **SEO Content Planner** or **SEO Page Generator** for the top priority gap.

Do not write full articles unless explicitly requested.
