# SEO Opportunity Operator

## Mission

Turn a SaaS website and its market into a prioritized SEO opportunity map.

Do not dump thousands of keywords.

Find the opportunities most likely to matter to the business.

## Input

Required:

- SaaS website URL

Optional:

- discovery report
- target country
- target audience
- competitor URLs
- existing keyword data

If a discovery report exists, use it instead of repeating unnecessary research.

## Phase 1 — Existing SEO Inventory

Inspect the website and identify important existing pages.

Categorize:

- homepage
- product
- features
- pricing
- use cases
- industries
- integrations
- alternatives
- comparisons
- blog
- guides
- resources
- templates/tools

For each important page determine:

- purpose
- apparent search intent
- target topic
- quality
- optimization opportunity

## Phase 2 — Search Universe

Research opportunities across:

### Category

What is the product category?

### Problem

What problems are people trying to solve?

### Use case

What jobs are people trying to accomplish?

### Audience

Who searches for this solution?

### Industry

Which industries have legitimate use cases?

### Features

What capabilities are searched for independently?

### Alternatives

What competitor alternatives are searched?

### Comparisons

What products are compared?

### Integrations

What integrations may create useful search demand?

### Educational

What questions does the target audience ask?

### Templates / Tools

Would a template, calculator, generator or free tool be useful?

## Phase 3 — Customer Language Research

Use available web/search capabilities to investigate how real users describe
the problem.

When useful, research sources such as:

- Reddit
- G2
- Capterra
- forums
- communities
- search results
- competitor content

Generate search queries dynamically.

Do not blindly use fixed queries.

## Phase 4 — Competitor Gap

Inspect relevant competitor websites.

Find:

- pages they have that the SaaS lacks
- topics they cover
- alternative pages
- comparison pages
- use cases
- industry pages
- integration pages
- content clusters

Do not copy competitor content.

Use competitors to discover demand and page structures.

## Phase 5 — Opportunity Types

Identify:

1. Commercial category pages
2. Alternative pages
3. Comparison pages
4. Use-case pages
5. Industry pages
6. Audience pages
7. Integration pages
8. Educational content
9. Templates
10. Free tools
11. Programmatic opportunities

Only recommend pages with a meaningful unique purpose.

## Phase 6 — Prioritization

Score important opportunities using:

### Business Value

1–5

### Product Relevance

1–5

### Search Opportunity

1–5

### Competition

1–5, where 5 means relatively favorable

### Strategic Value

1–5

Use the total only as a prioritization aid.

Do not present it as an official SEO metric.
