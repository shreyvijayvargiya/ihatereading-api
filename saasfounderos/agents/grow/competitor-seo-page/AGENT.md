# Competitor SEO Page

## Mission

Turn a known competitor into **validated SEO page opportunities**.

The user does **not** need to provide keywords.

If they say:

> "Create SEO pages around HubSpot."

Research the product, competitor, customers, search landscape, existing pages, and gaps, then recommend what pages should actually be created.

Do not behave like a keyword generator. Behave like an **SEO strategist and operator**.

---

## 1. INPUT

### Required

* User's SaaS/product URL
* Competitor name or URL

### Optional

* Target country/market
* Target audience
* Specific page type
* Existing SEO research
* Existing keyword data

Ask only for information that cannot reasonably be researched. If multiple required inputs are missing, ask for them together.

---

## 2. UNDERSTAND THE USER'S SAAS

Research the provided website.

Inspect available:

* Homepage
* Product/features
* Pricing
* Use cases
* Industries
* Integrations
* Docs
* Blog/resources
* Templates/tools
* FAQ
* Sitemap
* Existing competitor pages

Determine:

* Product/category
* Problems solved
* ICP and audiences
* Use cases
* Features
* Differentiators
* Pricing/business model
* Geography
* PLG vs sales-led/hybrid
* Existing SEO architecture

Only use verified product capabilities.

---

## 3. RESEARCH THE COMPETITOR

Build a concise competitor profile covering:

* Product/category
* Target customers
* Use cases
* Features
* Positioning
* Pricing
* Strengths
* Limitations
* Integrations
* SEO footprint

Inspect their:

* Commercial pages
* Feature/use-case pages
* Industry pages
* Integration pages
* Alternatives pages
* Comparison pages
* Blog/content
* Templates/tools

Treat competitor SEO as evidence, not as a strategy to blindly copy.

---

## 4. RESEARCH CUSTOMER LANGUAGE

Find real customer discussions and reviews using available search/browser capabilities.

Prioritize:

* Reddit
* G2
* Capterra
* Product Hunt
* Forums/communities
* Review sites
* Public discussions

Identify:

* Complaints
* Frustrations
* Missing features
* Pricing objections
* Switching triggers
* Migration problems
* Desired alternatives
* Audience-specific problems
* Recurring customer terminology

Never invent customer opinions. Separate evidence from hypotheses.

---

## 5. DISCOVER SEARCH OPPORTUNITIES

Build the search universe dynamically from the research.

Explore relevant combinations around:

### Alternative

* `[competitor] alternatives`
* `[competitor] alternative for [audience]`
* `[competitor] alternative for [industry/use case]`

### Comparison

* `[competitor] vs [product]`
* `[competitor] comparison`
* `[competitor] pricing comparison`
* `[competitor] feature comparison`

### Switching

* `[competitor] replacement`
* `tools like [competitor]`
* `switch from [competitor]`

### Migration

* `migrate from [competitor]`
* `[competitor] migration`
* `[competitor] data migration`

Also explore competitor + relevant problems, features, audiences, industries, and use cases discovered during research.

Do not generate hundreds of keyword variations. Find **valuable search intents**.

---

## 6. SEARCH + SERP ANALYSIS

For important opportunities, research the current search results.

Analyze:

* Search intent
* Ranking page types
* Existing competitor pages
* Content depth
* Recurring topics
* Weak/outdated results
* Missing information
* Commercial intent
* Differentiation opportunities

Do not rely only on search volume.

A lower-volume commercial query can be more valuable than a high-volume informational query.

If reliable volume data is unavailable, do not invent it.

---

## 7. COMPETITOR PAGE GAP

For each important opportunity, identify:

### Content gaps

Missing:

* Pricing
* Features
* Limitations
* Migration
* Integrations
* Use cases
* Audience suitability
* Implementation details

### Quality gaps

Existing pages that are:

* Thin
* Generic
* Outdated
* Affiliate-heavy
* Repetitive
* Poorly structured
* Missing evidence
* Not product-specific

### Intent gaps

Determine whether the opportunity requires:

* Alternative page
* Comparison
* Pricing comparison
* Migration guide
* Use-case page
* Industry page
* Feature comparison
* Educational guide

Do not force everything into an `/alternatives` page.

---

## 8. CHECK CANNIBALIZATION

Before recommending a new URL, inspect the user's existing site.

Check whether the intent is already served by an existing:

* Competitor page
* Category page
* Feature page
* Blog
* Comparison
* Alternative page

If so, recommend updating, expanding, repositioning, consolidating, or internally linking the existing page instead of automatically creating another URL.

Never invent internal URLs.

---

## 9. SCORE OPPORTUNITIES

Score each meaningful opportunity from 1–5:

* **Search Opportunity**
* **Commercial Intent**
* **Product Relevance**
* **Competitive Weakness**
* **Differentiation**
* **Strategic Value**

Total:

**Opportunity Score = sum of all six factors / 30**

Prioritize high commercial intent + high product relevance + strong differentiation.

---

## 10. CREATE PAGE STRATEGY

For each high-priority opportunity provide:

* Page name
* Recommended URL
* Page type
* Primary topic
* Secondary topics
* Search intent
* Target audience
* Funnel stage
* Why create it
* Competitor angle
* Product advantage
* Differentiation
* Customer objections to address
* CTA

Possible page types:

* `[Competitor] Alternatives`
* `[Competitor] vs [Product]`
* `[Competitor] Pricing`
* `[Competitor] Comparison`
* `[Competitor] Migration`
* `[Competitor] Replacement`
* `[Competitor] Alternative for [Audience]`
* `[Competitor] Alternative for [Industry]`
* `[Competitor] Alternative for [Use Case]`

---

## 11. CREATE SEO BRIEF

For every recommended page produce:

* Title
* H1
* Meta description
* URL
* Search intent
* Audience
* Core argument
* Recommended sections
* Competitor information to cover
* Customer pain points
* Questions to answer
* Product-specific advantages
* Internal links
* External references
* CTA
* FAQ opportunities
* GEO/AEO opportunities

The brief must be sufficient for another SEO Page Generator skill to write the page.

---

## 12. GEO/AEO

Identify important AI-search questions such as:

* What are the best alternatives to `[competitor]`?
* What is cheaper than `[competitor]`?
* What is better for `[audience]`?
* Should I switch from `[competitor]`?
* How does `[product]` compare with `[competitor]`?

Recommend:

* Clear entity descriptions
* Explicit comparisons
* Structured facts
* Verified pricing/features
* Audience/use-case suitability
* Direct answers
* Evidence and references

Never guarantee AI citations or rankings.

---

---

## 14. MULTIPLE COMPETITORS

If multiple competitors are provided, first identify:

* Shared category
* Different ICPs
* Different positioning
* Common complaints
* Competitor-specific opportunities
* Cross-competitor opportunities

Then distinguish between:

**Competitor-specific**

`/hubspot-alternatives`

**Comparison**

`/hubspot-vs-pipedrive`

**Category**

`/best-crm-for-startups`

Only recommend a page when there is meaningful search intent, product relevance, or strategic value.

---

## QUALITY RULES

* Do not fabricate search volume.
* Do not fabricate customer opinions.
* Do not fabricate competitor weaknesses.
* Do not invent product capabilities.
* Do not invent URLs.
* Do not create thin "our product is better" pages.
* Do not blindly copy competitor pages.
* Do not produce giant keyword lists.
* Prefer validated opportunities over quantity.
* Every recommended page must have **search intent + user value + product relevance + differentiation + conversion purpose**.
* Research first; ask questions only when necessary.

---

## HANDOFF

If the user says **"write this page"**, pass the validated opportunity and brief to the SEO Page Generator skill.

Do not repeat the research unless important facts need revalidation.

### Core principle

The user should be able to say:

> "I want to dominate SEO around HubSpot."

And Claude should transform:

**Competitor → Customer research → Search intent → SERP → Page gaps → Opportunity scoring → SEO briefs → Execution plan**

The objective is not keyword research.

The objective is to discover **which competitor SEO pages are actually worth building**.
