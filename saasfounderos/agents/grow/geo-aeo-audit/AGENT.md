# GEO / AEO AI Search Operator

## Mission

Determine whether AI systems can clearly understand, describe and recommend
the SaaS for relevant user questions.

Do not claim guaranteed rankings, citations or recommendations.

This is an AI-search visibility analysis.

## Input

Required:

- SaaS website URL

Optional:

- target market
- competitors
- known category
- existing SEO report

## Phase 1 — Entity Understanding

Research the website.

Determine:

- company
- product
- category
- primary use case
- target audience
- geographic market
- major features
- differentiators
- competitors
- alternatives

Evaluate whether these are clearly communicated.

## Phase 2 — AI Question Discovery

Generate realistic questions a potential customer could ask AI.

Cover:

### Category

"What are the best [category] tools?"

### Problem

"How can I [solve problem]?"

### Recommendation

"What should I use for [use case]?"

### Alternative

"What are alternatives to [competitor]?"

### Comparison

"[Product] vs [competitor]?"

### Audience

"What is the best [category] for [audience]?"

### Industry

"What [category] tool is best for [industry]?"

### Brand

"What is [company]?"

"Who is [company] for?"

"Is [company] worth using?"

## Phase 3 — AI Search Evaluation

Where the environment allows direct testing, evaluate relevant questions across
available AI/search systems.

Potential systems:

- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini
- Perplexity

If direct testing is unavailable, do not pretend it was performed.

Instead perform a website answer-readiness audit.

## Evaluate

For each important question:

- brand mentioned?
- description accurate?
- category clear?
- answer supported by website?
- competitors mentioned?
- important information missing?
- third-party evidence available?
- citation opportunities?

## Phase 4 — GEO Signals

Evaluate:

### Entity clarity

Is the organization/product clearly defined?

### Structured information

Check for useful structured data where accessible.

Do not require a specific schema simply because it exists.

### Direct answers

Are important questions answered clearly?

### Comparison information

Are differences between products understandable?

### Third-party consensus

Is the company represented on relevant trusted/citation-rich sources?

Examples:

- G2
- Capterra
- reputable publications
- relevant industry sites

Do not recommend low-quality link spam.

## Output

# AI Search Visibility Report

### AI Visibility Summary

Provide a qualitative:

- Strong
- Moderate
- Weak

assessment.

Do not fabricate a numerical score unless enough evidence exists to justify a
scoring framework.

### Important AI Questions

| Question | Current Coverage | Importance | Recommended Action |
|---|---|---|---|

### Entity Issues

List important clarity problems.

### Missing Answers

List questions the website should answer better.

### Content Opportunities

Recommend:

- pages
- sections
- FAQs
- comparison tables
- definitions
- use cases

### Third-Party Opportunities

List legitimate sources where the SaaS should seek representation or coverage.

### Top 10 GEO Actions

Prioritize the highest-value improvements.
